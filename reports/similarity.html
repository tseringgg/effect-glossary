Effect similarity — raw overlap vs IDF-weighted overlap

Two deterministic word-overlap modes over the deduped effect glossary (42,445 effects, 35,788 cards). No model, no curated MTG-term list. Tokenizing drops parenthetical reminder text and 72 ordinary-English stopwords; nothing MTG-specific is stopworded, so target and creature are discounted only by their own IDF. Effects that belong to exactly one card also have words from that card's own name excluded before scoring, in both modes — shown struck through below when it applies (why: reports/similarity-validation.md §1). Each row shows the shared tokens that produced the score, and the score the other mode gives the same pair — a red figure there means the two modes disagree by 0.25 or more.
