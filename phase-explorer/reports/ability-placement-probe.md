# Per-ability scoring probe: would it rescue the unplaced cards?

Investigation only. Nothing is placed, nothing is re-clustered, no threshold or frozen file changed. Generated by `src/report_ability_probe.py` from `build/ability_probe.json`, `ability_probe_extra.json` and `ability_probe_named.json` (scorer: `src/ability_probe.py`, `run_ability_probe.py`, `probe_extra.py`, `probe_named_cards.py`).

## Bottom line

- **The diluted-blend mechanism is real and almost universal in the 0.70-0.80 band:** 951 of 953 cards have an ability scoring >= 0.90 against some leaf.
- **But the usual shape is not 'one perfect match + one stray'.** Of the 737 two-ability cards in that band, **718 have BOTH abilities >= 0.90, against two different leaves**, and only 19 fit the one-perfect-plus-one-unmatched story. These are genuine two-effect cards. Per-ability scoring does not find one better home for them; it finds two (or three). It is a multi-membership layer, not a re-placement.
- **Scores are bimodal** (almost everything is >= 0.99 or < 0.5), so the threshold barely matters: 2,392 / 2,393 / 2,396 cards are rescued at 0.90 / 0.80 / 0.70.
- **A third of the rescues are low-information:** 712 of the 2,392 rescued cards match only generic leaves (a bare `eff:Draw`, `eff:Token` or `eff:Mana`, or a leaf of 500+ cards). Restricting to specific matches keeps 1,524.
- **Recommendation: build it, narrowly.** See the last section.

## 1. The scorer

No feature space is invented. `src/ability_probe.py` rebuilds the clustering's own: the same vocabulary (insertion order over the 26,972 clustered + noise cards, 3,706 tokens), binary presence x IDF (`ln((1+n)/(1+df)) + 1`), L2 normalisation and leaf centroids (mean of clustered members' vectors, L2-normalised), exactly as `build_placements.py` does. One vector per ability item uses `card_features()`'s token rules restricted to that item (effect + target for abilities, triggers and replacements via `execute`; mode + `affected` for statics); each is scored by cosine against all 657 centroids.

Reproduction checks, all exact: the union of an item's tokens equals `card_features()` for all 22,348 clustered faces (0 mismatches); the blended score and best leaf of all 4,175 cards in the unplaced group reproduce the placement file's numbers (0 mismatches).

Ability items in clustered faces: **30,077** (abilities 14,279, triggers 10,510, statics 3,816, replacements 1,472). **116 produce no tokens** ({'replacements': 110, 'triggers': 6}); none has tokens outside the vocabulary. In the unplaced group: 7,791 items, 18 with no tokens.

## 2. Validation on already-clustered cards (done before trusting anything else)

**Single-ability exactness: passed.** 16,227 clustered faces have exactly one token-bearing ability (16,126 have exactly one ability item at all). In every one the ability vector and the card vector are bit-identical, and so are all 657 scores: **0 differences.**

Best-ability score vs own leaf, decile histogram (0.0-0.1 ... 0.9-1.0): [7, 0, 4, 2, 12, 29, 188, 1296, 1075, 19176].

What this says. For a single-ability card the two scores are the same thing, as they must be. For a **multi-ability** card in its own leaf, the best single ability scores >= 0.90 in only 59% of faces against 72% for the blended vector, and in 2,607 multi-ability faces the best ability beats the blend. The existing leaves were built from blended vectors, so a card clustered into a combined leaf (Draw + Token, say) is a worse fit per ability than per card. **Per-ability scoring is a good detector of single-effect leaves and a poor fit for combined ones**, so it should not touch cards that already have a placement.

## 7. Clean-parse caveat

Every card in the unplaced group is `clean` by definition (the pipeline filters partial, unmodelled and corrected cards out before placement), so the meaningful split inside it is **clean and unflagged** versus **clean but flagged by the condition-drop detector** (a clean parse the detector says lost a condition; 311 cards, 7.4%).

| band | unflagged cards | rescued >= 0.90 | flagged cards | rescued >= 0.90 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.70-0.80 | 857 | 855 (99.8%) | 96 | 96 (100.0%) |
| 0.50-0.70 | 643 | 622 (96.7%) | 66 | 66 (100.0%) |
| <0.50 | 2,364 | 692 (29.3%) | 149 | 61 (40.9%) |

Of the 4,299 rescuing abilities, **184 (4.3%) are themselves the item the detector flags** (on 180 cards). **No conclusion changes if only unflagged cards are counted**: the 0.70-0.80 band is 855 of 857 either way (99.8%), and the overall rescue rate is 56% unflagged against 72% flagged (flagged cards skew toward multi-ability text). A clean-but-wrong ability (Spark Double) is invisible to this check, so a high score cannot certify an ability; it can only say the ability looks like a leaf's.

Extra population (not asked, but the caveat's other side): of the 5,708 partial and unmodelled cards, 2,844 have an ability free of gap nodes and 2,586 have one scoring >= 0.90. That would place a card on the part the parser understood while hiding the part it did not. Not recommended.

## 8. Recommendation

**Build a per-ability layer, narrowly, as an additive 'also fits' layer (a card may appear under several leaves, never in place of one).**

- **Population:** only the unplaced 'Parsed, no close group found' cards with a blended score of **0.50 or more** (1,662 cards). 1,363 of them have at least one *specific* match. Do not touch the 2,513 cards below 0.50: only 161 have a specific match, 529 match only generic leaves, 1,760 match nothing, and most are one-ability cards the scorer cannot help.
- **Threshold: 0.90.** Scores are bimodal, so 0.80 and 0.70 add four cards and only invite noise.
- **Per ability, not per card:** list each ability >= 0.90 as 'also fits leaf N (score)', up to the card's own abilities; do not claim the card 'belongs' to a leaf. Most would show two or three leaves.
- **Exclude generic matches from the claim** (single bare token, or a leaf of 500+ cards) or show them as a muted 'also draws / makes tokens'. 712 rescued cards (17%) would otherwise be placed on a bare `eff:Draw`/`eff:Token`/`eff:Mana` leaf and tell a tester nothing.
- **Never apply to cards that already have a placement,** to modal spells without handling the modes (216 cards in the group), or to the 184 abilities the detector flags individually.
- **Do not extend to partial or unmodelled cards.**
- **What it would and would not fix.** It would give 1,363 currently suggestion-only cards a real, specific 'also fits' link, and explain the 0.70-0.80 band honestly: they are two-effect cards, not near misses. It would not change the 1,783 cards that match nothing, and it inherits the token scheme's blind spots: counter type, everything after a chain's first effect, replacement declines, and mode-level abilities.

### Surprises

- The hypothesis was right about the mechanism and wrong about the shape: two perfect matches in different leaves, not one perfect match and noise.
- 43% of the group (1,783 cards) has no ability matching any leaf at 0.90, and 1,615 of those are one-ability cards: genuinely unusual effects, not dilution.
- Clustered cards are themselves not always above the floor: Tireless Tracker sits in its leaf at 0.779 blended; 5% of clustered multi-ability faces score under 0.71 per ability against their own leaf.
- 18 abilities in the group (and 116 in the clustered set, mostly replacements) produce no tokens at all.
- Leaf-label problems surfaced by the scorer, not caused by it: 'Bounce removal' on a graveyard-return leaf, 'Tapper' on 'enters tapped', and a derived phrase 'Exile target ___' on the cast-with-flash leaf.

### Limits of this probe

The 30-ability false-rescue sample is a sample, and the verdicts are one reader's. 'Specific' and 'generic' use fixed cut-offs (3 tokens, 300 and 500 cards) chosen to be easy to state, not tuned. The detector flag is the only quality signal beyond the pipeline's own. Nothing was run on cards the pipeline excludes, other than the partial/unmodelled count above. `build/ability_probe.json` came from one run; the report generator is deterministic.
