Branch review queue

Borderline placements left for a human instead of guessed — see also card explorer · branches.md · sector map
This queue is persistent and asynchronous. Nothing here blocks a build, and nothing is auto-resolved. A decision is written to corrections/branch_review.json and read back by src/branch_leaves.py on the next rebuild, so assign and reject entries never come back. Defer keeps an entry queued but marks it as already seen. Re-run python src/branch_leaves.py to apply decisions.
