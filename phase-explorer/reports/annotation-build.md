## The 17 abilities with no condition word, and why they were flagged
They are not annotated (a card cannot show a condition that is not in its text). I read the detector's quoted clause against the held ability for all 17: **in every one the clause the detector quotes is not in the held ability's own text; it comes from elsewhere on the card**: another face or mode (Chalice of Death: the held ability is "Target player loses 5 life", the clause is the card's other ability, "if you have at least 10 life more than your starting life total..."; Nezumi Graverobber: a flip clause; Odious Witch and Avacynian Missionaries: a transform trigger), another ability of the card (Ashmouth Dragon, Cecil, Dark Knight, Kuon's Essence), or a later part of the same card text that the held ability's text does not carry (Finest Hour's "if it's the first combat phase of the turn"). I did not sort the 17 further than that. So the flag is a card-level attribution to an ability that does not hold the condition; they stay in the pile and none is lifted. (Counted from the data; the reading is mine, over all 17, not a sample.)

A search of the page (`browse.html`, `findcard.js`, the other pages and scripts) finds neither "closest group" nor "also fits": those links exist only in the archived card-level view, which the page links to for reference. ## Which checks are independent of the generator
**Independent** (they read finished files against the card text or count mechanically): the seeds; the quotes (`check_annotation_build.py` compares every ability and every condition shown to the card's oracle text); the counts, the four figures and the reconciliation, recomputed from the data in the Python checker and from the data the page holds in the browser; the hashes and `git diff -- archive`; the two identical builds; the headless Edge run (the page's behaviour, the badge style, the phone-width measurement).
**Not independent:** my hand reads (the 40 + 20 placements, the 20 + 20 shared-verb reads, the 20 clause re-reads, the 40 gap-test reads): the same model that wrote the generator judged it, and the samples are too small to show a rate.
