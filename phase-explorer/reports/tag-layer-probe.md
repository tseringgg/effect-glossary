## Part 3. Quality (my hand reads; 30 groups and 40 cards cannot show a rate)
**30 tag groups (seed 20261036, set before computing; 10 each from 5-20, 21-100, over 100; up to 8 members read first; my read of what the members do written to `build/tag_probe_reads.json` before the tag was shown).** Verdicts after the reveal: **24 coherent, 5 loose, 1 incoherent (3.3%)**. Bar (more than 25% incoherent): not tripped. By stratum (coherent / loose / incoherent): 5-20: 7 / 3 / 0; 21-100: 8 / 1 / 1; over 100: 9 / 1 / 0 (rows in `build/tag_probe_result.json`).
Loose: `unprinted-token` (members create tokens; the name does not say so), `protects-artifact` (about half), `mill-each` (3 of 5 mill), `enters-in-company`, `noncreature-typal` (reads as the opposite of what members do). Incoherent: **`type-errata`** (a templating note; heterogeneous members). Coherent but jargon: `imprint`, `ferocious`, `sneak-creature`. The over-100 groups are the broad classics (`spot-removal` 336, `removal-destroy` 141, `attack-trigger`, `draw-engine`, `symmetrical`): coherent and large, so they sort the pile but narrow nothing.
**40 unreadable cards, one tag each (seed 20261037, set before computing; a seeded pick among the tags that would put the card in a group of 5 to 100; card text read first and my fair description written before the tag was shown): 27 right, 11 loose, 2 wrong.** Bar (more than 5 wrong turns the stratum off): not tripped. Wrong: Scarwood Bandits and Ghouls' Night Out, both under templating tags (`type-errata`, `untracked-indefinite-effect`) that say nothing about what the card does. Loose: for example `repeatable-removal` on Fire Juggler and Words of Wind, `offcolor-ability` on a hybrid-cost ability, `refund` on Drain Power. The wrong and many loose ones are the same kind: **design, templating or errata tags**. A denylist of that kind of tag would remove most of them (not tested).
**Circularity.** These tags are the community Tagger tags of the same bulk file that the card_function_search project turned into its otag glossary (its glossary's descriptions and slugs are Scryfall's own; it generated its candidate worksheet from this file). So the tags are *not independent of that vocabulary*: they are the vocabulary. They are independent of our parser and of our taxonomy (nothing here comes from phase.rs or the signatures), so tag grouping is a second opinion on cards our parse cannot read, not a check of the parse. Where it matters for the label: (1) the layer must say "community tag", not "verified" or "classified", because the only thing that "validates" a tag name is the same tagging; (2) the group-quality read above is my reading of the card text against the name, which is independent of the tags; (3) I did not use the glossary's prose to judge any name; (4) tags must never be used to measure the parser or the other layers.
**Name quality (measured, existing defect and claims checks over the 611 groups of 5 to 100 with the slug humanized, "pp" -> "+1/+1").** Hard defects (double space, code words, dangling ends, wrong a/an): **0**. Vocabulary flags (a word found in no card text: jargon such as "anthem", "impulsive", "saboteur", "crime", "typal"): **346**. Claims hits (a verb in the name that fewer than half of the members' text contains): **18** (for example "Long term impulsive draw", "Mill opponent", "Surveil"). **358 of 611 names (59%) would need a rewrite** for a tester. 370 of the 611 have a Scryfall description that could serve as a one-line explanation, not as the name (descriptions are short, some link to other tags, and are the same community text).

## Part 4. Design (not built)
**Where it sits.** A separate section in the tree under the loose layer: "Browsed by community tag" with its own badge (a fourth style, e.g. a double-underlined "community tag" pill, distinct from the dashed "nearby" chip, the solid keyword and broad badges, the striped tentative badge and the dotted "Condition not read" badge). Families by the hand-written 16 only as a navigation aid (option 3a), groups by tag (option 1) under them; groups of 5 to 100 only; the over-100 tags as a search filter, not a group.
**Wording.** "Grouped by community tag. Not checked against the card's parsed text." On a row: the tag's humanized name, the Scryfall description when it has one, and the date of the tag snapshot. Rewritten names (358 of 611) go in a hand-written overlay file, never generated.
**Switches.** A page checkbox "Show community-tag groups" (on by default, stored like the others); build flags `TAG_LAYER` and `TAG_STRATUM_NO_READABLE_ABILITY` / `TAG_STRATUM_OTHER_CAUSES` (each alone), `TAG_MIN_GROUP=5`, `TAG_MAX_GROUP=100`, `TAG_FLOOR=20`, and a tag denylist file. Method in the data: `community_tag`.
**Counting a card once.** A card is "tag-placed" if it has at least one tag group of 5 to 100 and no placement in any earlier layer; it appears under every such group and counts once. Order: placed, tentative, annotation (condition stratum), tag, unorganized, not cards. Reconciliation (estimate for the stratum, arithmetic exact): **27,590 + 2,093 + 960 + 1,258 + 1,282 + 5,738 = 38,921** for the stratum of the 1,300; with all causes, 27,590 + 2,093 + 960 + 2,472 + 68 + 5,738 = 38,921. The gap-test stratum stays its own line (the 160 cards are in the 2,540; with it on they leave the tag layer's population: 2,380, 2,312 reached).
**A sixth figure.** On the 92.3% base, its own line, never blended: for the 1,300 stratum **31,901 = 96.1%** (30,643 + 1,258; basis: the with-annotation figure plus the unreadable-ability cards that sit in a community-tag group of 5 to 100). With all causes it would be 33,115 = 99.8%, which is exactly the problem: a figure that high says "nearly everything is organized" when the groups are community-tag groups that are 6 to 360 cards wide; the line should say "browsable by community tag", not "organized", and the page should not show it as a percentage next to the first three.
**The 1,300 in "Cards we couldn't read yet".** Add a tag filter and sort to that list: each row gets its tag chips (groups of 5 to 100, median 4 a card), the list can be filtered to one tag (350 tags form a group of 5 or more among the 1,300 alone; they reach 1,255) and sorted by tag popularity. The biggest tags there: `copy-creature` 52, `alternative-cost` 51, `digital-only-mechanics` 49, `multi-removal` 40, `untracked-indefinite-effect` 40 (a templating tag: deny), `drawback` 40, `repeatable-lifegain` 39. A card keeps the "couldn't read" reason line; the tags sit beside it.
**No tag, and a stale snapshot.** The 27 cards (1.1% of 2,540; 21 of the 1,300) with no functional tag stay in the pile with the text "no community tag yet"; nothing is guessed. The page shows "Tags from Scryfall's Tagger, snapshot 2026-10-14" in the layer's header and on each card row; the build writes the snapshot date, the tag file hash and the count of cards released after it; the page turns the line amber when the snapshot is older than a set age (for example 60 days; the old snapshot was 90 days old and missed 25 of 27 cards released after it). The refetch is a separate manual step (`src/fetch_tags_probe.py` shows the pattern: index first, download only when `updated_at` moved); slugs are never stored, tag UUIDs are.

## Part 5. Recommendation
* **Grouping option: one group per tag (option 1), groups of 5 to 100.** Option 2 (tag sets) reaches 1.1% of the pile and is unusable. Option 3a/3b are navigation aids on top of option 1, not a replacement: the hand-written families map 520 of 1,131 tags cleanly (the 20 read: 11 right, 7 loose, 2 wrong) and the hierarchy is coarse but is the same data.
* **Stratum to include first: the 1,300 cards with no readable ability.** It is the stratum with no other home, the 40-card read passed its bar (27 right, 11 loose, 2 wrong of 40; the bar was more than 5 wrong), and coverage there is as good as anywhere (98.4%). **Hold the other four causes (1,240 cards)** until each has its own 40-card read: I only read cards of the 1,300, and those cards already have a loose group or a partial annotation.
* **Expected cards moved (measured counts, not correctness): 1,258 of the 1,300** (96.8%); 1,259 with the gap stratum on (the one-card difference is a group crossing the 100 limit). All causes would be 2,472 of 2,540 (97.3%). **Correctness is an estimate:** from the 40-card read, about 95% of tag placements are a fair or loose description (38 of 40), about 5% wrong (2 of 40); 40 cannot show a rate, and the reads are mine (not independent of the generator).
* **Before building:** a tag denylist for design, templating and errata tags (`type-errata`, `untracked-indefinite-effect`, `offcolor-ability`, `refund`...) read and tested on a fresh sample; a hand-written name overlay for the 358 names that need one; the label "community tag" everywhere; the snapshot date on the page.
* **Which numbers are measured:** snapshot dates and sizes, UUID comparison, the filter counts, all coverage counts, the Part 2 group counts, reach and reconciliation arithmetic, the name-scan counts. **Estimates:** the 95% / 5% correctness (40 reads), the claim that a denylist would remove most bad tags (untested), the 358 rewrites (a mechanical vocabulary check is stricter than a tester would be), the figure for any stratum other than the 1,300.
* No overlapping counts: the tag layer's population is the pile after the three earlier layers; the gap stratum is a separate line; the 1,258 are not in any other layer.
