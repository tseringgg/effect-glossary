# Tester note

Kept in the repo so the wording and the numbers stay in step with the build. `python src/check_tester_note.py` compares the claims below with
`build/ability_taxonomy*.json` and `reports/browse.html` and lists any that no longer hold. Update this file and rerun the check whenever a build
changes the headline, the scope, or what the page offers.

---

## What this is

A browsing tool for finding Magic cards by what they do. You explore groups of cards that share an effect (removal, ramp, card draw and so on) instead of searching for a name you already know.

It's built on an open-source rules parser that is still in progress. Most cards are read accurately, but some aren't, and the tool shows you where.

## What to expect

- Oracle text is on every card. If a group or detail looks off, the card's own text is the source of truth.
- Groups are made from individual abilities. A card with two effects can appear under two groups. Cards the tool shows under a group are placed by the ability shown next to them.
- Some cards are placed by one ability while another part of their text isn't understood yet. The card page shows the part that wasn't read.
- Some groups are marked as broader than their name says. The parser doesn't record every detail (who draws, for example), so those groups can hold more than the name suggests.
- Group names can be imprecise. The cards inside a group are right more often than its label is. If a name confuses you, tell me.
- About 73% of constructed-legal cards are in a group, and about 83% counting the broad ones. The rest are in "Not yet organized," and each one says why. They're still findable.
- Some cards in "Not yet organized" are also shown under "Tentative groups" in each family (about 2,100 cards; about 90% of the card set counting them). They are grouped by effect type but not fully checked, so treat them as hints. A switch at the top hides them.
- Some cards in "Not yet organized" have an ability the tool held back because it did not read part of it, usually a condition such as "if you control a Swamp". Under "Condition annotations" in each family, that ability is shown in the group it would belong in if the condition were ignored, with the condition quoted on the card and a dotted "Condition not read" badge. The condition may change what the ability does, even to the opposite, so treat the placement as a hint (about 960 more cards; about 92% of the card set counting them). A further 160 cards, whose ability was read but another part of the card was not, are on their own line with a "Card part not read" badge (about 93% with them, kept separate). A switch at the top, "Show condition annotations", hides all of it.
- Cards the tool could not read at all (about 1,200 of the 1,300 in "Cards we couldn't read yet") are also shown in a section called "Grouped by community tag": groups made from Scryfall's community tags, with the tag, the date of the tag snapshot and the line "Not checked against the card's parsed text." The tags are volunteers' labels, so treat them as hints for browsing, not as a check of anything (about 96% of the card set counting them, on its own line, never added to the other figures). The same list has a tag filter. A switch at the top, "Show community-tag groups", hides it.
- A few cards have been placed in a group by hand, with a solid blue "by hand" badge. That is a person's judgment, not a checked placement: it is counted on its own line ("placed by hand"), never added to the other figures, and a switch at the top, "Show hand edits", hides all of it.
- A "Keyword" filter on the Cards tab and a "By keyword" list show which cards carry a keyword wherever they are placed. It is a filter, not a placement, and it changes no group and no figure. The parser's keyword list (172 names) is narrower than Scryfall's (836 names, which also include actions and ability words such as Scry and Landfall).
- A few groups are defined by hand (a dashed "hand group" badge, for example "Wins, loses or draws the game"). Their rules only suggest cards in the edit panel; a card is in one only if someone placed it there.
- Some tentative cards carry a "sibling" badge: the ability has exactly a group's signature but the grouping rule left it out because it is the plain variant. These are not checked, are counted in no figure, and a switch at the top, "Show sibling placements", hides them.
- In "To place" there is a "Batch view": cards with their suggestions and a checkbox each. Nothing is placed until you press Accept; one click is at most 100 placements, all or nothing, with its own id, and "Undo this batch" takes it all back. Suggestions are not checked, so a batch can contain loose ones. The tentative, tag, sibling and annotation group pages have a "Batch review" to confirm or reject ticked cards. A batch-placed card says so in Find a card, and the "placed by hand" line shows how many were placed in a batch. A switch at the top, "Show batch actions", hides all of it.
- Every card with rules text now has suggestions in the edit panel, including cards that are already placed (as other places it could also go). Some come from comparing the card's lines with the rules lines of each group's cards. Those are marked "low confidence" with the line that matched, because words can match without the meaning matching. In the batch view a row led by such a suggestion is only ever in the review band and is never ticked for you. They are suggestions only, never a placement, and cards with no rules text have none.
- Twelve more hand groups, with the dashed "hand group" badge, give a home to cards no other group takes: Attractions, the draft, ante, conspiracies that start in the command zone, face-down creatures, extra votes, landwalk, cast only after another spell, no counters, library-search locks and hands revealed. Their rules only suggest, show the card's own sentence, are never ticked for you in the batch view, and a card is in one only if someone placed it there.
- Twenty-five more hand groups cover mechanics no other group has: the Siege choice, the initiative, controlling another player's turn, Cataclysm effects, Teamwork, the Alchemy words "perpetually", "boon", "specialize" and "intensity", tickets, suspect, collect evidence, forage and others. They only suggest, show the card's own sentence, and a row led by one of them is never ticked for you.
- In the edit panel, groups with near-identical names (for example several "Exile cards from the top of a library..." groups) now share one block that shows the best two and "+N similar groups" under it; keyword blocks are offered for more cards (landwalk by land, keyword pairs, "other combinations"), and a bare common keyword (flying, haste) ranks below groups with real evidence. Nothing about what is ticked or listed in the batch view changed.
- On a group page, a second tab, "Possible matches (N)", lists cards the tool only suggests for the group (and, on a community-tag group, cards that carry its tag). They are not members, are counted in no figure and never appear on the group's own tab; Add places one by hand (Undo takes it back). The tab has a floor slider (0.70, down to 0.50) and a tick for already-placed cards, and is hidden when a group has none.
- The search box at the top is "Find a card or group". Results are in two sections, Cards and then Groups, with jump links; a group is searched by its name, by a short list of common terms ("alias": for example "wrath" or "board wipe" lead to the groups that hold those cards), and then by its one-line description (labelled "matched: description"). Groups that match only by the family they sit in are under one collapsed row ("matched: family"). A word that is not a real word is corrected ("did you mean"); a real word never is ("wraith" is not turned into "wrath"). An alias that only partly fits says on the row what it leaves out. It searches names, aliases and descriptions of groups, never the text of cards. Community-tag and tentative groups appear only while their switches are ticked. What you type in this box is logged on this computer (the words, how many results, whether you opened one) so the common terms can be improved; the page says so beside the box. Under the results, "Didn't find it?" is one click that tells us what you were looking for (the query is logged in its own field). Some terms are "suggested aliases" drafted from Scryfall's own tag spellings; they answer only to their exact words and are always listed below a name match. A phrase of three or more words that matches all but one word of a group's name is listed last, labelled "matched: most of your words".
- Some groups are shown together on one **merged page** (badge "merged page"): one section per group, each with its own name, count and limit note and an "open on its own" link. This is display only; the number at the top counts a card once even when it is in two of the groups, so it is a display figure, and every group keeps its own count and placements.
- A card in the search results opens the card in its group: the group's page with the card pinned at the top as "You searched for this card", "Also in" chips for its other groups, and the card highlighted (and scrolled to) in the list, even on a late page of a big group. It picks the group in this order: a checked placement, then a tentative one, a community tag, a loose group, a keyword block, and last a group you put it in by hand. A card that is not yet organized (and a card with no group the page can open, for example one you removed from its only group) opens its card page as before; the "card page" button on a result always does. The address carries the card, so a pasted link shows the same pin. It places nothing.
- Silver-border, playtest, acorn and similar cards are left out, since they aren't meant for constructed play. Find a card tells you when a card is one of these.
- Cards released since April are included but parsed by an older version of the parser, so a few newer mechanics may show a gap.
- Tester site, filter bar (feature `TESTER_FILTERS` in `reports/config.js`): on a group page, colour, type and mana value chips and a sort (best match, mana value, name) narrow the list. Chips of different kinds combine with AND, choices inside one kind with OR, and "Showing N of M" says how many remain. Colour is the card's colour, not its colour identity, so a Forest is colourless here. Mana value has a "0" chip and a "No cost / unknown" chip: the data cannot tell a free spell such as Mox Jet from a card with no cost, so lands, some free spells and some back faces share it. X counts as 0.
- Colour match (feature `COLOUR_MODES`): under the colour chips, "Any of these" (the default: the card has at least one of the chosen colours), "Includes all" (it has every chosen colour), "Only these" (every colour it has is one of the chosen ones; a colourless card counts only if the colourless chip is on) and "Not these" (it has none of them). The colourless and multicolour chips are extra conditions in the last three: "Includes all" with W and multicolour means white cards of two or more colours. Multicolour is greyed out under "Only these" (pick the colours instead, for example W and U). On a phone the four modes are one drop-down.
- Two-faced, split and adventure cards: without the feature `FLIP_FACES` the filters use the first face the page lists, not the whole card (a transforming or modal card is read from the face the page shows, sometimes the back; a split card from its first half; an adventure card from its creature; its mana value is that face's cost, so 222 cards differ from the card's total cost, and 172 from its colours). With `FLIP_FACES` on, colour and type read both faces (the union; mana value stays the page face's), and a card in the list only because of its other face says "matched on its other face: NAME".
- Flip (feature `FLIP_FACES`): the large card view of a two-faced card has a Flip button. It opens on the card's first face (not necessarily the face the group page lists) and swaps the picture, name, cost, type line and rules text. Only transform and modal cards have a picture for each face; for adventure, split, aftermath, flip and prepare cards one picture shows both halves, so Flip there swaps the text only ("other half"). The card search also finds a card by the name of its other face, and says which face matched.
- Card types (feature `TYPE_GROUPS`): under "By card type" in the left column, and in the search box, there is a page for every card type, supertype and subtype that 5 or more cards have (11 types, 4 supertypes, 306 subtypes today; for example Creature, Legendary, Saga, Equipment, Elf). A card is on the page if either of its faces has the word on its type line; one that is there only through its other face says "via other face". These are filters and pages, not placements: they are in no group, change no count and no figure, and are not offered as "Narrow by group" chips. In the filter bar a "Subtype" row adds any of them to a group page (a card must have any of the chosen ones). Changelings are creatures of every type but do not say so on their type line, so they are not on the creature-type pages. A search for "saga" or "sagas", "elf" or "elves", "creature", "tribal" (Kindred) opens the type page first.
- "Narrow by group": under the filters, chips list the other groups that share cards with the list you are looking at, with a count. Ticking one keeps only the cards that are also in that group; ticks stack with AND, counts are recomputed on what is left, and a chip that would not narrow anything (90% or more of the list) is hidden. It uses the same group lists as the group pages (it never adds a card to a group), the member groups of a merged page are not offered, and near-duplicate groups are left out. Dashed chips are grouped loosely. Without the feature `NARROW_NO_CUT` a group needs at least 3 shared cards. With it, 2: the chips run from the most shared cards down, a smaller group before a bigger one when the counts are equal, chips that would keep exactly the same cards are shown once, a group whose name only restates the page's (every word of the shorter name is in the longer one) is left out, 12 are shown and "Show more" adds 12 up to 60, and "Show single-card groups" adds the groups that share only 1 card. The row is always there, with its count in the folded line on a phone.
- Ability types (features `ABILITY_TYPE_PAGES` and `ABILITY_TYPE_FILTERS`): under "By ability type" in the left column, and in the search box, there are 42 pages for the kinds of ability a card has: triggered (by trigger: enters, dies, attacks, upkeep and so on), activated (by cost: tap, sacrifice, mana, loyalty and so on), static (anthems, cost reduction, evasion and so on) and replacement (enters tapped, enters with counters and so on). Each row shows the card's own line for that ability. They are pages and filters, not placements: they are in no group and change no count or figure. The filter bar gets Trigger, Cost and Kind rows (choices inside a row OR, rows AND with each other and with the other filters). The parser does not read every card: ability-word activations such as "Max speed -" are parsed without a cost and are missing from the cost pages, 6.5% of the page entries (1,995 of 30,915) are only partly read and say so, and about 53 cards whose text looks like a trigger or an activated ability but has none parsed are left out on purpose.
- Narrow by topic (feature `BROAD_NARROWING`): "Narrow by group" opens with 15 topics (Card draw, Counters, Tokens, Sacrifice, Removal and so on) with a number each. A topic is a set of groups, and a group can be in several; the number is how many of the cards showing are in any group of it, so it is not the sum of the groups in its dropdown. Pick a topic to keep those cards, then optionally add specific groups from its dropdown (any of the chosen inside a topic; all topics together). Groups that fit no topic are reached with "Show all groups", which shows the flat list of groups as before. Topics are filters, not placements: they change no group, count or figure.
